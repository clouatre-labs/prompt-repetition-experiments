# Exp5 Pre-registration

Pre-registration record for Exp5 (PHP grammar scout task with a model factor). The protocol is `protocol.md`; `AMENDMENTS.md` is empty at deposit.

## Deposit contents

The deposit is the `experiments/exp5-php-grammar-powered/` package: the seven files below plus `README.md`, `gen_recipes.py`, `run.py`, `plans/` (`main.csv`, `pilot.csv`, `pilot-h45-recheck.csv`, `smoke-haiku45.csv`), `scorer/` (scorer, tests, fixtures, vendored snapshot and pilot evidence), `analysis/` (analysis, stats, pricing, tests) and `tests/`. `recipes/recipe-*.yaml` are generated by `gen_recipes.py` from the plan and `runner-prompt.md` and are not deposited. Metadata is `.zenodo.json`.

`label-map.json` is produced by `run.py` at seal time and is never hand-written: it does not exist at deposit, so its sha256 is written here by the owner at seal time, and the deposit timestamp is earlier than its earliest `sealed_at`.

`AMENDMENTS.md` is empty at deposit: it contains only its title and the statement that there are no amendments. Any later change to the protocol is appended there with a date.
